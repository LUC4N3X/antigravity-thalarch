Severity guidance:
- CRITICAL: likely compromise/data loss with reachable path.
- HIGH: serious exploitable boundary failure under realistic conditions.
- MEDIUM: meaningful weakness requiring preconditions or limited impact.
- LOW: hardening or low-impact weakness.
- INFO: non-blocking observation.

Confidence:
- confirmed: direct code/data-flow evidence or reproduction;
- high: complete path with one externally dependent assumption;
- tentative: plausible but missing a required link — do not present as confirmed.
